# Lint Goblin

![Lint Goblin](../img/lint-goblin.svg)

> Lives in the whitespace between your tokens and feeds on trailing spaces, unused imports and the odd stray semicolon. Harmless on its own, which is why it never is.

## 🎖️ Variants
|  | Variant | From | HP | Reward | Says |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| <img src="../img/lint-goblin.svg" width="72"> | **Lint Goblin** | level 1 | 14 | 50 XP, 12 'kens | *"Mmm, trailing whitespace."* |
| <img src="../img/lint-goblin-senior.svg" width="72"> | **Senior Lint Goblin** | level 5 | 21 | 75 XP, 18 'kens | *"I wrote half your style guide. The other half is a TODO."* |
| <img src="../img/lint-goblin-skip-level.svg" width="72"> | **Skip Level Lint Goblin** | level 10 | 31 | 110 XP, 26 'kens | *"Let's align on tabs versus spaces. Offline. Forever."* |

- **Senior**: Has been here longer than the linter config and has opinions about your variable names.
- **Skip Level**: Only appears when someone two levels up needs 'a quick sync'. Nobody has left one cleanly.
