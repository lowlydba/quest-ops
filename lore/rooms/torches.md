# The Torch Hall

![The Torch Hall](../img/room-torches.svg)

> Two sconces flicker on a wall of old stone, the last evidence of a lighting migration that never finished. Everything here is warm, and nothing is documented.

Rooms are scenery. Every foe stands in one, picked from a hash of the run's seed, and it changes nothing about the fight or the dice.
