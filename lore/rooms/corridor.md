# The Carpeted Corridor

![The Carpeted Corridor](../img/room-corridor.svg)

> A long stone passage with a red runner that nobody has vacuumed since the last migration. The far end is dark in the way unmonitored services are dark.

Rooms are scenery. Every foe stands in one, picked from a hash of the run's seed, and it changes nothing about the fight or the dice.
