# Sudo Shiv

![Sudo Shiv](../img/sudo-shiv.svg)

🟣 **Legendary dagger · weapon for Rogues only · +7 damage**

No spells, but every hit lands harder.

> Asks for no permission and gets none, which turns out to be the same as having them all. Armour is mostly a policy, and policies have exceptions.

**Effect:** Your first attack each fight ignores armour and cannot miss.

**Set:** part of [Zero Trust](../README.md#-sets), with the Lockfile Locket.
