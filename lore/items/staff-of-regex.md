# Staff of Regex

![Staff of Regex](../img/staff-of-regex.svg)

🔵 **Rare staff · weapon for Wizards only · +1 damage**

A weak weapon, but only a Wizard holding a staff can cast spells.

> Matches every spell on the first try. About one cast in six also matches the caster, because nobody anchored the pattern.

**Effect:** Spells cost 1 MP less (never below 1), but about one cast in six matches too much and also hits you.

**Set:** part of [Dependency Hell](../README.md#-sets), with the Lockfile Locket.
