# Force Merge

![Force Merge](../img/force-merge.svg)

🔵 **Rare mallet · weapon for Fighters only · +4 damage**

No spells, but every hit lands harder.

> Pushes past a red check with the confidence of someone holding admin rights. The branch protection rule is still there; it just has a new exemption.

**Effect:** Once per fight it ignores one failing result: your first miss becomes a hit, or the first hit against you is waved through, whichever comes first. It does not overrule fate on the last round.
