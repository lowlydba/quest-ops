# Local Bludgeon

![Local Bludgeon](../img/local-bludgeon.svg)

🟡 **Uncommon bludgeon · weapon for Fighters only · +0 damage**

No spells, and a gamble every swing.

> It worked on my laptop. One swing in four works on everyone else's machine, too. The rest of the time, it finds the user.

**Effect:** Each round there is a 25% chance of a one-hit KO. Otherwise the swing backfires and you take the damage.
