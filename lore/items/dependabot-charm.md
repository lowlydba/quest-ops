# Dependabot Charm

![Dependabot Charm](../img/dependabot-charm.svg)

🟡 **Uncommon charm · accessory, any class**

> Opens a pull request every time your luck goes stale. It is usually right, and it is always loud about it.

**Effect:** Once per fight, a missed attack is rerolled. Dependabot bumps the roll.
