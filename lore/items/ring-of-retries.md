# Ring of Retries

![Ring of Retries](../img/ring-of-retries.svg)

🔵 **Rare ring · accessory, any class**

> Whisper 'again' and the world rewinds. It works three times. Nobody remembers who owned it on the fourth.

**Effect:** On a passing run, round 3 always ends the fight in your favour. The first two rounds are harder (-2 to hit). It cannot rescue a failing run.

**Set:** part of [Retry Loop](../README.md#-sets), with the Hard Reset.
