# 📖 Spellbook

Only Wizards cast, and only with a staff in the main hand. Spells unlock with hero level, and a Wizard picks from the ones unlocked each round. Fights last three rounds, and the last one always ends with Jade. The Staff of Regex takes 1 MP off every cast.

| Spell | Unlocks | MP | Effect | Lore |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jade** | 1 | 2 | A plain bolt of damage. About half the time it leaves the foe Weary, which changes nothing. | The first spell every Wizard learns, because it compiles on the first try. It does what the label says, and sometimes the foe looks tired afterwards. |
| **Confuse** | 2 | 2 | No damage. The foe stumbles and its reply misses. | Feeds the foe a stack trace with no line numbers. It stumbles for a turn, which is the usual reaction. |
| **Hard Block** | 3 | 2 | No damage. A ward absorbs the foe's reply. | A required review from someone who is on holiday. Nothing gets through until the round is over. |
| **Refactor** | 4 | 3 | No attack this round: the staff morphs into a random other weapon. Next round you attack with it, quirks included, then it reverts. Rounds 1-2 only. | Renames the staff, extracts a weapon from it and swears the behaviour is unchanged. The new weapon is gone again by the next round, as with every refactor. |
| **Supply Chain Attack** | 5 | 4 | Two hits in one round. The second hits for +1 per link that landed before it. | Hits the foe, then the foe's dependencies, each link a little harder than the last. Nobody audited the second strike either. |
| **Pre-commit** | 6 | 5 | No attack this round: you gather energy. Next round's attack is a guaranteed critical hit. Rounds 1-2 only. | Spends a whole round running hooks and stays very still while it does. Whatever comes out next is formatted, linted and guaranteed to be critical. |
| **Zoltraak** | 7 | 4 | One big hit: about double a normal hit's damage dice. | An old spell with a very short syntax and no known flags. It is one big hit and no subtlety, which is the point. |
